How to play

Your Prime builds your base. Put Extractors on glittering ore veins for metal and build Solar Arrays or Wind Turbines for energy. Everything is paid for gradually while it is built, so keep income ahead of spending.

Factories make constructors and armies. Constructors unlock the Advanced Forge, whose Archwrights build tier-2 economy, super-weapons and the Colossus. Destroy every enemy Prime to win.
